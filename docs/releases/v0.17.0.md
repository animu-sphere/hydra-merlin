# v0.17.0 - Gaussian contribution culling and CPU Catmull–Clark

**Release date:** 2026-11-25 (JST)

**Tag:** `v0.17.0`

## Completed scope

- Vulkan and Metal offer opt-in conservative Gaussian contribution bounds.
  They use the raster alpha floor and the projected conic to reject whole
  Gaussians before sorting and reduce Gaussian-tile pairs. Counters cover
  rejection, pair savings, saturation and tile early termination.
- Experimental CPU OpenSubdiv converts uniform Catmull–Clark levels 1–3 to
  triangles for native Mesh and Hydra. It evaluates indexed UVs and
  subdivision tags, prepares normals and tangent frames on the refined mesh,
  and reuses the refinement plan for points-only edits.

## Verification evidence

Exact and conservative Adaptive produced 120 byte-identical moving viewport
image pairs on Apple M3 and 80 on RTX A5000. Higher contribution floors of
`2/255` and `4/255` changed images, composited alpha and picking IDs in the
tested cases. Exact remains the default. The timed sequences used unpinned
clocks and power, and the total GPU cost varied by scene. See the
[paired sequence evidence](../reports/delivery-history.md#v0170-paired-gaussian-sequences-and-quality-thresholds--2026-11-23).

On Windows, the Core fixtures agree with OpenSubdiv Far for expanded
positions and indexed UVs. RTX A5000 testing covers native rendering and
usdview, including authored cages, complexity changes, points-only edits
and steady frames. Teapot and Kitchen_set compare uniform level 1 against
the polygon path. See the
[subdivision measurements](../reports/delivery-history.md#cpu-catmullclark-scene-verification--2026-11-25).

Windows Release passed all 26 Core cases with OpenSubdiv enabled and all
26 with it disabled. The Vulkan/Hydra configuration passed all 88 cases.

## Compatibility and limits

Renderer settings schema 4, backend contract 10 and shader ABI 17 require
rebuilt SDK consumers and shader artifacts. CPU subdivision requires a build
with `MERLIN_ENABLE_OPENSUBDIV=ON`. Unsupported subdivision schemes and
non-manifold cages fall back to polygons. See the
[support matrix](../reference/support-matrix.md#subdivision) for verified
configurations and the remaining Metal subdivision coverage.
