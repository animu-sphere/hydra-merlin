# v0.16.8 - Open Chess Set opaque materials

**Release date:** 2026-11-23 (JST)

**Tag:** `v0.16.8`

## Completed scope

- Hydra Forward reads direct MaterialX Standard Surface base-color and
  tangent-space normal images, plus metalness and specular roughness from
  constants or direct linear UV0 images. Hio decodes the 8-bit JPEG and PNG
  channels, and Hydra shares textures and equivalent samplers across
  materials.
- The camera light and authored distant lights use the same isotropic GGX
  specular lobe on Vulkan and Metal. Metalness controls reflectance and
  reduces diffuse energy. Roughness controls highlight width. The dome
  supplies diffuse illumination and a separately controllable background.
- The unmodified Open Chess Set stage is the usdview acceptance scene.
  Session-layer probes isolate four authored inputs on the board and both
  sets of pieces. A controlled surface measures the effect of constant
  metalness and roughness with camera and distant lights under both dome
  settings.

## Verification evidence

On Apple M3 with OpenUSD 26.08, 31 Tier 0/HgiMetal image pairs agree
pixel-for-pixel. All 12 per-input scene edits change the rendered image.
The fully metallic highlight expands from 159 to 487 pixels at half maximum
when roughness changes from 0.4 to 0.6. Both directional sources show the same
widths with the dome on and off. Core passes 25 cases and the standalone
Metal SDK passes 37 cases without skips.

The [Forward validation guide](../guides/forward-validation.md#open-chess-set)
describes the captures and their output files.

## Compatibility and limits

Backend contract 8, shader ABI 15 and GPU Scene ABI 3 require rebuilt SDK
consumers, shader artifacts and binaries. SDK assets cover Windows/Linux Core
and macOS Apple Silicon Core + Metal. Vulkan, Hydra and the MaterialX compiler
remain source-build products.

Alpha blending, the pawn tops' tinted transmission, specular dome reflections
and broader MaterialX graphs remain outside the supported subset. See the
[support matrix](../reference/support-matrix.md#forward-lighting-and-output).
