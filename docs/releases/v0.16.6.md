# v0.16.6 - Tangent frame and normal mapping

**Release date:** 2026-10-18 (JST)
**Tag:** `v0.16.6`

## Release theme

v0.16.6 implements and validates the tangent-frame and normal-mapping part of
the [surface geometry contract](../design/surface-geometry.md). Core prepares
Mesh tangents for native and Hydra meshes with one priority. Every Vulkan and
Metal Forward entry builds the tangent frame in the shared Slang surface
context and decodes and applies linear tangent-space normal textures through
it. UsdPreviewSurface normal maps now reach that path. Representative quality,
material parity and MaterialX `normalmap` remain the next v0.16.x milestones.

## Completed scope

- Core prepares Mesh tangents with an authored → generated → derivative
  priority. Generation uses texture coordinate set 0 with MikkTSpace
  conventions and welds corners by point, texture coordinate, normal and
  handedness, so mirrored UV islands and seams are not averaged. Hydra reads a
  float3/float4 `tangents` primvar with every interpolation.
- Conventional, bindless, GPU Scene, GPU-driven and generated Vulkan Forward,
  and native Metal Forward, transform tangents with transform handedness,
  orthogonalize them against `Ns_base`, fall back to the screen-space
  derivative frame and negate the frame on double-sided back faces.
- Linear normal textures decode as `2t - 1` with a material green flip and xy
  scale and apply through the frame before the hemisphere rule.
- UsdPreviewSurface `normal` connected to `UsdUVTexture` becomes the material
  normal texture when its scale/bias express that decode. Normal maps on
  geometry without texture coordinates are disabled per draw and reported.
- `tangent` and `bitangent` debug views join the surface views.
- The analytic surface fixture grows to 21 cases and the usdview normals
  fixture adds normal-map probes; the GPU capability workflow runs both.

  on Tier 0 and HgiMetal. All 60 centers, including normal-map sign, green

## Compatibility and limitations

`DrawVertex` grows to a 64-byte stride with a tangent. `MaterialConstants`
gains the normal-map policy and table indices, `GpuMaterial` the normal
texture, and the conventional Vulkan material set a normal texture at
binding 1. Rebuild SDK consumers, shader artifacts and binaries for backend
contract 6, shader ABI 13 and GPU Scene ABI 2.

The MaterialX `normalmap` node remains unsupported and diagnosed; MaterialX
tangent-space normal maps are not claimed until v0.16.8. UsdPreviewSurface
normal maps whose scale/bias express another encoding are disabled.
Displacement, parallax and subdivision-specific tangents are out of scope.
Representative-scene evidence comes from Kitchen_set viewpoints on Apple M3,
which have no normal maps; it is not a pixel-exact gate or interior-quality
evidence.

SDK assets remain Windows/Linux Core and macOS Apple Silicon Core + Metal.
Vulkan, Hydra and MaterialX runtime products remain source-build products.
