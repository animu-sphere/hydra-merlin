# Code of Conduct

hdMerlin contributors and maintainers should be able to participate without
harassment. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and
project-related events or channels when representing the project.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and constructive, including when reviewing a proposal or
  reporting a defect. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make room for different experience levels and backgrounds. Ask for context
  before assuming intent, and accept corrections without personal attacks.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately intimidate
  others. Do not share someone's private information without consent.
- Do not use project spaces for unwanted sexual attention, repeated personal
  contact, or disruptive behavior after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

For abusive content hosted on GitHub, use GitHub's
[report-content process](https://github.com/contact/report-content). If a
maintainer offers a private contact method on their GitHub profile, you may
also use it to report a project conduct concern. Do not post private details
about someone in a public issue. Vulnerabilities follow the separate private
process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will consider the context, protect reporters' privacy as far as
practical, and avoid public discussion of sensitive reports. Depending on the
behavior, they may ask for a change in conduct, edit or remove content, close
or lock a thread, or restrict participation. Repeated or serious violations
may result in a permanent restriction. Decisions can be revisited by contacting
the maintainers privately through an available profile contact method.
